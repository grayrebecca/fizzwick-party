Fizzwick started in 2011 at a market stall in Peterborough with three boxes of balloons and a helium tank borrowed from a friend, and since then we've grown into one of the UK's favourite online party shops, shipping over two million balloons, a frankly silly amount of bunting and more confetti than we care to count to birthdays, weddings, baby showers, office leaving dos and the occasional very enthusiastic dog birthday, and we still pack every order by hand in our warehouse where the radio is always on and someone is usually testing a party popper that they definitely shouldn't be, and if you'd like to know more about how we choose our suppliers and why we switched to paper packaging in 2019 you can read our sustainability promise, or if you're planning something big like a wedding or a corporate event our events team can put together a custom bundle with free samples, so click here to get in touch and we'll get back to you within one working day, and don't forget that everything comes with our no-quibble 30-day returns because we know plans change and sometimes the theme changes three times before the big day.
